# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- First public release.
- Hold-to-record mimic clips. Your voice chat is muted while you record.
- Mimics are synced across all players. The player whose voice is used hears nothing.
- Enemies avoid using the voices of players who are near them. Clips are played with random timing, pitch, volume, fragments and repeats.
- Each level picks a random share of its enemies (half to all) as mimics, and several can talk at once in different parts of the level.
- Saved clips are kept outside the mod profile, so profile exports never include your voice.
- Works with hosts running the separate LethalMimics AI add-on (GitHub only): on-screen notice you can dismiss, and `ShareVoiceWithHostAi` to opt out.
